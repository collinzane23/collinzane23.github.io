Why Ohio

Ohio is home. Galion sits in the middle of the state, which puts Columbus, Cleveland, Toledo, Akron and Dayton all within about ninety minutes to two hours. Most of what we do is here: hospitals and clinics in Mansfield and Marion, manufacturers across Richland, Crawford and Wayne counties, banks and credit unions in every county seat, and thousands of homes with a filing cabinet that's been full since the nineties.

How we work in Ohio

Galion, Bucyrus, Mansfield and Marion are same-day or next-day. The rest of the state is scheduled by region — north to the lake, west to Lima and Toledo, east to Akron and Canton, south to Columbus — and we'll tell you when we're next headed your way. Every job is on-site, witnessed, and ends with a certificate of destruction.

Ohio's rules

Ohio doesn't have a single statute that tells private businesses how to dispose of paper records; the federal rules — HIPAA, GLBA, and the FACTA Disposal Rule — do the heavy lifting. Two Ohio laws matter on top of them. Ohio's breach-notification law (R.C. 1349.19) requires businesses to notify residents when their personal information is exposed, and paper found in a dumpster counts. And the Ohio Data Protection Act (R.C. 1354) gives businesses a legal safe harbor against data-breach lawsuits if they follow a recognized security framework — and documented, secure disposal of records is part of every one of those frameworks.

Questions from Ohio

How soon can you be here?

Galion, Bucyrus, Mansfield and Marion are usually same-day or next-day. Everywhere else is scheduled by region — call and we'll tell you when we're next headed your way.

My town isn't listed. Do you still come?

Almost certainly. The areas above are where we go most; tell us where you are and we'll quote it.

Is there a travel charge?

Not on recurring service — scheduled accounts never pay travel. One-time jobs have one flat charge, quoted up front: $75 within an hour of Galion, stepping up with drive time farther out, and waived on jobs of 100 boxes or more. The full table is on our pricing page.
